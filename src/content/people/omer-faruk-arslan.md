    I am an MSc student in the Department of Biology at Dokuz Eylül University. My research centres on evolutionary biology, cytogenetics and three-dimensional geometric morphometrics.

    In our project supported by the TÜBİTAK 2559 – Bilateral Cooperation Programme with Serbia (MoESTD), I study the relationship between the rich chromosomal diversity of blind mole rats (Nannospalax) and their skull morphology. Using landmark-based geometric morphometric analyses on 3D digital models of specimens, I ask how closely chromosomal divergence among cytotypes is paralleled by morphological change. In short, I trace the evolutionary history of these remarkable subterranean animals at the level of both chromosomes and morphology.

    Beyond the hours spent in the lab with digital skull models and morphometric data, observing nature as it unfolds and documenting those moments through photography and video is an inseparable part of research for me. Taking my scientific curiosity into the field and behind the viewfinder, I try to understand the forms of living things not only at the micron scale but also within the habitats they live in.
